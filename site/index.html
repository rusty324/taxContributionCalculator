What this does and doesn't model
  • Uses 2026 federal brackets, standard deduction, child tax credit ($2,200), 0/15/20% capital gains bands, self-employment tax, additional Medicare tax, net investment income tax and the 65+ senior deduction.
  • Wages for the rest of the year are your current paycheck × remaining pay periods, plus any bonuses you list. Bonuses are assumed withheld at the 22% supplemental rate.
  • The Step 3 suggestion (used when you're over-withheld and have no Step 4(c) extra left to remove) is exact under the IRS percentage method: each $1 of Step 3 lowers withholding by $1 ÷ pay periods per year.
  • Not modeled: state tax, the refundable Additional Child Tax Credit, the earned income credit, the QBI deduction, AMT, capital losses, phase-outs of other credits. Missing QBI and refundable credits makes the estimate conservative (it may suggest slightly more withholding than needed).
  • Social Security wages are approximated by taxable wages when computing SE tax, and Medicare wages likewise for the additional Medicare tax.
  • This is an estimate, not tax advice. For a second opinion use the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator.
